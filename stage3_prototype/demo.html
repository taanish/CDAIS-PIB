From 363 releases to 99 system records

Every release is read against the rulebook and sorted. Most turn out not to be about AI at all — a keyword caught them, but the text is a false match or a passing buzzword. What survives is genuine AI activity, and inside it, the actual deployed or planned systems.

What this is — and isn't — yet

  • A prototype on one month. 363 of the 5,893 AI-flagged releases (2024–2026). The point is that the schema holds and the process runs end to end, not full coverage.
  • Machine-labelled, not yet human-checked. Claude Sonnet applied a fixed rulebook. The 99-record shortlist is the queue for a human review pass — not a finished catalogue.
  • Accuracy not yet measured. The next step is a hand-labelled sample to score the machine against, which becomes the defensible accuracy figure.
  • Every record is checkable. Each carries a verbatim quote (verified against the source text) and a link to the original release — nothing is asserted without a citation.
